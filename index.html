Methodology

Five audit lanes.
One complete picture.

Every audit I conduct runs through five proprietary lenses. Each lane surfaces a different layer of the customer experience — together they reveal what a business cannot see from the inside.

01Digital Customer Experience
First impressions, navigation flow, visual hierarchy, calls to action, mobile responsiveness, and load experience. I audit the digital journey the way a first-time visitor would — without the context your team takes for granted.
02Physical and Hospitality Experience
Arrival impressions, environment and atmosphere, staff interactions, signage and wayfinding, and the post-visit experience. For service businesses, retail, and hospitality — the physical journey matters as much as the digital one.
03Trust Signal Auditing
Perceived credibility, social proof, security indicators, brand consistency, and the micro-moments that build or erode customer confidence. Trust is either present before the decision — or the decision never happens.
04Conversion Moment Auditing
The exact moments where a customer decides to act — or does not. I identify the friction points, unclear value propositions, and buried calls to action that are costing businesses conversions they do not know they are losing.
05Onboarding and Registration Experience
The first experience a new customer has after deciding to engage. Drop-off during onboarding is one of the most preventable forms of churn — and one of the least audited. I map every step from signup to first value.
